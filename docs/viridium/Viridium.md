A pseudorealistic worldgen mod for GTNH inspired by real-life national parks around the globe, with realistic terrain, geology, and ecology for different biomes and terrain settings. Viridium intends to redo every existing part of the overworld and Nether biomes and replace every worldgen feature from Biomes O’ Plenty, IC2, RTG and Pam’s Harvestcraft while also incorporating Natura and Forestry into its worldgen. What we intend to create here is a worldgen mod so beautiful that people will want to move out of their personal dimensions and back into the overworld. Viridium intends to jam-pack its biomes with as much life as it can, creating a world that feels real.

Thanks to the concurrent development of Cubic Chunks and Distant Horizons, Viridium can be developed with a much grander scale than current terrain-generating mods do. However, I also want to make sure to keep the ease of navigation that vanilla tends to hold over worldgen mods which tend to amplify vertical change, making worlds more difficult and less fun to navigate.

One important question I’ve been asked is about migration from the old worlds. In this case, the answer is to leave both Viridium and the old worldgen mods in the game for a couple versions, but having Viridium only overwrite other mods’ blocks in old worlds, not changing anything about worldgen, and in newer worlds, completely throttle and replace worldgen.
