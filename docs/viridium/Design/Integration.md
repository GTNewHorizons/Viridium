Ore generation is set to be massively overhauled. The existing ore veins will be somewhat switched around, still retaining their natural generation styles, which are reflavored to be Hydrothermal or Placer deposits at the surface, but with the addition of Cubic Chunks, the underground may now stretch thousands of blocks deep, giving room for new, rare, and massive ore veins the deeper you go.

This mod is not Terrafirmacraft, nor is it meant to be TFC or emulate it in any way. TFC mechanics such as biome-exclusive ores, cave-ins, and stuff like that will not be a thing.

## Stone Age Progression

Minor progression changes will be made to the pack’s stone age. Instead of asking for oak logs, the questbook will ask you to collect small logs, which can only be crafted into 1 plank at a time, but can be broken twice as fast, and by hand. When you make an axe out of this wood, you can now chop down entire small trees at once, and you can begin cutting down large trees. Extremely tough woods, such as Ironwood and Redwood, will require a mining level of 2 (Bronze) to break, but offer a cheaper but more easily broken alternative to the iron pickaxe (mining level 3) if you choose to use it.

## Changes from Vanilla

Vanilla grasses and flowers will be completely reworked, using less generic species of plants to replace most of them. There are dozens of species of grass and flowers already, so to mitigate potential inventory issues with this system, all grasses will drop generic plant matter, and some stone age tools such as the Wicker Basket (which holds any flower the player picks up) to help manage flower collection. 

Trees will no longer drop saplings. Instead, breaking leaves gives a chance to drop a tree’s seed, fruit, or cone, which is placed in the ground to start growing the tree. The sapling will grow from this seed, and from there it can be moved around to decorate and grow trees.

I already currently have hundreds of minerals drawn out, these will probably have to be whittled down to a more reasonable number for the pack, otherwise the materials system may be inadequate.
