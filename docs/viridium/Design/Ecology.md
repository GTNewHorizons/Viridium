Plant life is getting a major overhaul in Viridium, to the tune of many common and unusual plant life native to the eco-regions and national parks that the mod pulls from. A few of Minecraft’s native plants will be replaced with more realistic variants. For example, the rose bush has been remade into an actual bush-type block, and lilac, which previously existed as a 2-block tall flower, has been remade into a thin tree-type block.


## Classifications of plants in Minecraft’s setting

### Full Trees

- Trees like the traditional oak, spruce, birch, etc. still are normal, behaving as 1-block thick trees, and producing 2 wood per log. Some trees, such as redwoods and baobabs, can be much larger, but still produce the same amount of wood per log.
- Full trees usually require at least 9 textures: wood planks, logs, stripped logs, fancy and fast leaves, a sapling, and seeds.
- **Example** - Cork Tree - Quercus robur

### Small Trees

- Smaller trees, such as the dogwood tree, are only as thick as a wall block (8 pixels thick), and produce 1 wood per log. Connected branches will generate in a lattice-like structure, similar to medium pipes. 
- Thin trees usually require at least 8 textures: wood planks, logs, bark (identical to the log sides except taking up the full block), fancy and fast leaves, a sapling, and seeds.
- To compensate for their poor yield, thin trees also grow significantly faster than large trees, and also suffer less of a penalty from growing in the wrong environment.
- **Example** - Wild Apple Tree - Malus sieversii

### Bushes/Shrubs

- Plants like berry bushes and rhododendron bushes generate as full, semi-transparent blocks with a layer of leaves connected by a connecting branch system in the middle, that connects similarly to tiny pipes. Bushes can be walked through slowly (sometimes damaging the player), reduce fall damage, and can be broken and crafted into sticks, but not wood planks.
- Bushes need at least 2 textures: leaves and branches, which are cross-shaped 2x16 textures, similarly to tiny wires.
- **Example** - Rose Bush - Rosa majalis

### Grasses

- Small plants that are disconnected from the grass blocks but do not drop themselves when broken without shears. While grasses are flowering plants by definition, they are not drawn with their flowering form unless said flowering form is an edible grain.
- **Example** - Sugarcane - Saccharum officinarum 

### Flowers

- Small plants that drop petals of their main color. Flowers are allowed to be more than one block wide. Certain flowers may drop edible leaves or petals when broken.
- **Example** - Snapdragon - Antirrhinum majus
#### Succulents

- Succulents are flowering plants adapted to desert conditions. When collected, they can drop petals or edible leaves if they are flowering or edible, or be crafted with an empty bottle to produce water. Succulents are also more specific with the conditions they can live in, requiring the exact conditions listed in their ecological profile in order to spawn.
- **Example** - Century Plant - Agave americana

#### Tree Ferns
- Tree ferns are very large ferns which grow woody blocks upwards. Cycads are also labeled under tree ferns despite being a gymnosperm, due to having similar growing characteristics. Tree Ferns may grow fronds on the sides of the wood blocks, while Cycads only have fronds on the top.

### Mushrooms

- Mushrooms drop themselves when broken, and can sometimes be placed on the side and top of blocks, having a different texture and model when they do so. Some mushrooms also have sapling-like properties, able to be grown into giant versions of themselves. 

### Grass Decorators

- Plants which decorate the ground floor of a biome and do not have a hitbox, instead generating as a modifying extension of the grass block, such as mycelium, podzol, and clover patches. They only drop dirt when broken, but can be sheared to separate the decorator and the grass block. When the decorator is placed down again on a dirt or grass block, it then becomes that decorated grass block again. Grass decorators allow for multiple plants on a single grass block.

### Vines

- Decorators which spawn on the top/side of a given block and grow down a certain set number of blocks or until they hit a surface they cannot spread to.

### Ferns and Cycads

- Fern and Cycad-type plants either sprout from the ground or grow over time, like bamboo. They have leaves sprouting out circularly from their base. Other plants that aren’t ferns or cycads may use this growth type as well.
- Tree Ferns and Cycads both have fronds, stems, and a bark block associated with them. Despite their similar appearance, palm trees are not a cycad. Fronds will very often be larger than 16x16 pixels. Non-fern plants, such as Monstera, which have a more large and radial morphology, may also use the Fern plant type, as may some smaller palms. Cycads have a spongy wood plank texture, while ferns and tree ferns have no planks at all.
- Cycads are grown using a “strobilus” which grows on the side of the plant when fertilizer is applied, or at random when growing, if in the right conditions.

### Crops

- Crops come in many different plant types. The fig grows from a large tree, olives grow from a small tree, raspberries from a bush, grapes from a vine, onions from a flower, wheat from a grass, etc. Ground crops either spawn from a “wild form” where their crop is harvested directly from a form not seen from regular crop farming, or just their final stage will generate in the world in an X pattern and not a # pattern. Root vegetables commonly have a wild form, while grass crops usually don’t.
- Due to the survivability importance of crops and edible fruit trees, crops often have a wider range than their native range would suggest, taking in ranges from plants of the same genus, or taking into account early cultivation routes.

### Minor/Unique Plant Types

- Growing Plants
- Plants such as bamboo, cacti, and sugar cane all grow block-by-block from the ground.
- Complicated Plants
- Some waterplants such as the Sacred Lotus have multiple parts which are stitched together to make the plant work. 
- Ivy
- Vine-like plants which spawn on the ground. They have a bush-like stem, as well as leaves which spread out from the stem. Due to the stem being able to be connected with other stems, the textures for ivy must have an 8-pixel wide base.

## Plant Spawning Conditions:

All plants have preferred temperature, humidity, and environment types, which is used to determine if a plant is successful when it tries to generate in a certain place. For temperature and humidity regimes, plants have a 70% lower chance of generating in an adjacent regime, and a 0% chance of generating in any regime more than 1 spot away. For example, a plant which has a 10% success rate of spawning in a Boreal region will have a 3% chance of spawning in a subpolar or temperate region. If this success rate is dependent on both humidity and temperature, these chances are additive, so a plant with a 10% success rate of spawning in a Boreal temperature and a Semi-Humid rainfall requirement will have a 20% success rate in semi-humid and boreal environments such as wet forests. Plants are a lot more picky when it comes to environment types, and will only spawn in the correct terrain setting. Lacustrine and Riparian environments allow for the spawning of plants with up to 2+ higher humidity requirements in a certain region.

## Mutating Flora

4 new tiered levels of mutate are being shipped with the mod, each capable of mutating a plant to a greater division, from the simplest mutate only being able to change a plant to another in the same genus, then to ones in the same family, order, and class,  with the greatest tier of mutate being able to transform plants as genetically unique from each other as wheat grass and palm trees. This leaves a few genetic outliers that you still will have to collect for yourself, such as Ginkgo biloba, the only extant species in its entire class. Trees will have to be transformed from their sapling stage.
