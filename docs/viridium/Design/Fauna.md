Some mobs will be added to add life to the barren Minecraft ecosystem. At minimum, this will include mobs added by Biomes O’ Plenty and the mobs that you can capture using traps from Pam’s Harvestcraft. That being said, mobs are still a secondary goal of Viridium. It would be nice to get at least one representative from each major Animalia phylum, especially ones that highlight new biomes that are being added. Megafauna will probably be the most represented section of Animalia, and some smaller animals will be sized up to help fit the scale of the world better.

## Time Period

Mobs in Viridium will consist of animals alive through the Holocene and Pleistocene, though the latter will be rarer, preferentially being located in rarer cold biomes to match the Pleistocene Ice Age they lived in. I think this allows for a more fantastical and varied atmosphere despite being vaguely anachronistic.

## Sessile Fauna

Sessile fauna are animals which, in adulthood, are permanently located in one location, moving only if necessary. Sessile fauna in the mod can work similarly to plants, being spawned down at a specific place and not moving except perhaps for limited animation cycles. Most sessile fauna are marine, and include things such as barnacles, brachiopods, coral, sponges, and certain mollusks.

## Active Fauna

Mobs should have representation from most major Animalia phyla to maximize biodiversity. Highlighted mobs will be well-known ones which play a major impact on the ecosystems and biomes that they live in.
