Subtle changes to immerse the player even deeper into the new world.

## Ambient Fauna

- Subtle, non-interactable flocks of birds will fly above the world, disappearing into the distance, or not appearing at all if there is too much pollution.
- Swarms of gnats and other bugs in humid, swampy biomes, dispersing when the player comes too close. Bugs will also flutter around designated blocks as a sort of particle effect, the same way firefly bushes work.
- Ambient fish? Probably not since I want fish to be interactable and fishable, but it’s an option.

## Weather

- Clouds should be algorithm-based rather than a regular texture, allowing for periods of clear skies at some times and dark, cloudy skies during snow, rain, and thunderstorms.

## Fog

- Mountain valleys may fill with ambient fog, but can be dispersed by torches and other light sources. This would work by adding an opacity value to air blocks which require certain conditions to be met to form.

## Water Aesthetics

- The water texture will be slightly modified to include a simple CTM lightening around the edges of solid water blocks to indicate how water becomes more transparent closer to the surface on shorelines.
- Water vapor is released at the base of waterfalls.
- Entities which fall or are thrown into water will release a splashing animation.
- Water will be updated to blend more smoothly and have dynamic coloring similar to modern Vanilla Minecraft post-1.13.2
  
## Falling Leaves

- Another backport from vanilla, certain trees will periodically drop leaf particles.

## Environmental Interactions

- Trees, especially, have different forms they might end up in due to environmental changes. 
- Trees in high-pollution areas will transform into Dead Trees, and those in high-radiation areas will transform into Irradiated Trees.
- Burning and being struck by lightning have a new wood type for each of their interactions. 
- Plant parts being run through different machines can cause new tree types as well, including Frozen trees.
