Reasonably-sized, dungeon-like structures will sparsely fill in the world upon generation, introducing players to various mechanics, mods, and blocks available through GTNH.

## Villages

Villages will have new houses and buildings based on the new biomes and wood types that they can spawn in. New villager professions could be added, though I’m not sure if that falls within the scope of the project.

## Trading Posts

Each Phytoregion will be able to spawn a ‘Trading Post’ structure in villages or rarely in the wild which comes with a backported Wandering Trader mob which sells a variety of plants and animal spawn eggs that may be associated with the region, but not actually native to said region. Examples include traders who sell pineapples in Hawaii, Callery Pear in the West Atlantic, camels in the Outback, and more. Wandering Traders can also spawn as they normally do, carrying a random selection of plants and animals in exchange for GT credits.
