Geology is a very complicated doctrine, as well as the one I am personally the most invested in, so I want geology to be as good as possible here. Viridium will take advantage of the upcoming Cubic Chunks-based world height, containing taller mountains and a deeper underground.

The first step of geologic world generation is to separate out continental terrain into “Provinces” after the terrain settings are decided. Using the combination of terrains and provinces, we can use those to make a weighted list of what rocks are able to generate, and in what forms (flat-lying strata, folded/inclined strata, plutonic bodies, large volcanic bodies, etc). Each rock will have a different erosion and weathering coefficient, which will help determine how tall mountains made of different materials are allowed to get. Lithologies will not determine what ore minerals are able to spawn where, but having ores spawn in their preferential rock types.

## Provinces

- Platform: A mostly flat layering of stratified sedimentary rocks 
- Shield: Exposed plutonic and meta-plutonic crystalline basement rocks making up an entire province.
- Orogen: A tectonically-altered province which has undergone the changes of mountain building, which can often include metamorphosis.
- Basin: A low area in between provinces that acts as a sediment sink.
- Large Igneous Province: A province afflicted by mass volcanism events, often covered entirely in stratified volcanic rocks.
- Extended Crust: The passive margins between oceanic and continental crust, made of complicated tidal and deltaic sedimentary sequences.

## Georegions

The rock which generates at the surface will have subtle effects on the biome that it spawns in. For the most part, this only reflects upon limestone, which can form lots of different features when it generates in a warm, humid biome.

Inventory management is something that many players worry about and the addition of likely hundreds of different rocks is not something that is going to alleviate those worries. To sort this out, rock units are going to drop cobblestones based solely on their composition, rather than their lithologies themselves, which also applies to machine processing of the rocks. This creates some funny scenarios where three different kinds of rocks can all be collected into the same rubble, such as quartzite, chert, quartz arenite (sandstone), chalcedony, and quartzolite all being able to be mined into the same cobblestone, called “Quartzose Rubble.” 


## Caves

Vanilla-style caves should still generate, but larger, specialized cave biomes should be restricted to karst limestone georegions. New cave biomes will exist, though these are mostly going to be inspired by more fantastical depictions of caves, including D&D’s Underdark and the Legend of Zelda’s Depths.
