For the vast majority of its existence, GTNH has just lacked oceans and ocean generation entirely. This makes the overworld feel endless and empty, while tons of important things go on in the ocean that we’re missing out on, like beaches, coral reefs, offshore oil deposits, hydrothermal vents, marine biology, guardian temples, and more. 

Oceans will contain geology and ecology unique to GTNH, and will include some optional, exclusive, and lucrative mineral sources should players decide to conquer the seas. This includes offshore oil deposits, where the amount of oil that a player will be able to find while prospecting for oil will be greater just offshore in the continental shelf. Oceanic terrain will also work differently than terrestrial terrain, being separated into continental shelves, continental slopes, abyssal plains, trenches, seamounts, and mid-ocean ridges. Biomes within these are separated by temperature, and these two variables will allow for different features to spawn in ocean biomes, such as coral reefs, kelp forests, and hydrothermal vent biomes.

Special biomes will form when oceans form alongside certain features, such as rivers. Wide, slow-moving rivers will form large delta systems, wall smaller and faster rivers will simply deposit into the ocean. These estuary systems will be a hot spot for biodiversity.
